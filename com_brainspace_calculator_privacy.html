Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Calculator is designed to perform calculations on your device. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require account registration and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party marketing services.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device so its features work as expected:

  • Calculation history and any notes you add to saved calculations.
  • Your selected light or dark theme preference.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to us or any third party. You can remove saved calculation history from within the app. Removing the app may also remove locally stored data, subject to your device settings and backups.

Children's Privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Third-Party Services

Calculator does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
